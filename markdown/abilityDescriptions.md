# In-game weapon abilities

The exact English descriptions currently used in the game, copied from `src/main/resources/assets/forgermod/lang/en_us.json`. This list includes 30 active abilities, 15 learnable passives, two intrinsic weapon passives, and nine material-hit effects. Unimplemented recommendations are excluded.

Weapon assignments include the base, Ruby, Amber, Amethyst and Jade variants. Item descriptions appear when holding Shift; augmentation cards use the shorter descriptions shown separately below. Descriptions are reproduced as written, including their current balance values.

## Active abilities

### Absolute Nightmare

**Weapons:** Nightmare Dagger

**Item tooltip:**

> Your next 2 dagger hits inflict Darkness I and Weakness I for 10 seconds (20-second cooldown)

**Augmentation card:**

> Your next two hits apply Darkness and Weakness for 10 seconds.

### Always the Gardeners

**Weapons:** Leafcutter Dagger, Overgrown Claymore, Verdant Axe

**Item tooltip:**

> Arm your next enemy kill for 60 seconds. It leaves a plant and a poison cloud for five minutes. Everyone in the cloud, including you and allies, receives Poison I for 20 seconds. Radius: 2/2.5/3/3.5 blocks. Switching cancels the garden. Cooldown after ending: 60/50/40/30 seconds.

**Augmentation card:**

> Your next enemy kill plants a five-minute poison cloud. Affects everyone for 20 seconds.

### Area Discharge

**Augmentation card:**

## Learnable passive abilities

### Chain Lightning

**Weapons:** Static Dagger

**Item tooltip:**

> Hits can chain up to 10 times, with a 50% chance per link. Each next hostile mob is within 10 blocks of the previous target and takes half your current weapon damage.

**Augmentation card:**

> Hits can arc between enemies for half weapon damage.

### Cold Bellows

**Weapons:** Infernal Claymore

**Item tooltip:**

> Successfully blocking damage with a shield ignites the attacker, including the shooter of a blocked projectile, for 3/4/5/6 seconds at levels I/II/III/IV. Hold this claymore in your main hand.

**Augmentation card:**

> Shield blocks ignite the attacking enemy or projectile shooter.

### Death March

**Weapons:** Deathwisper Dagger

**Item tooltip:**

> Dagger damage rises as the target loses health, up to double damage

**Augmentation card:**

> Deal more damage as the target loses health, up to double.

### Distance Tax

**Weapons:** Claymore of the Void

**Item tooltip:**

> A successful hit marks one enemy for 10 seconds. Then its distance from you deals 0.5/0.75/1/1.25 damage per block at levels I/II/III/IV, capped at 40 damage. Repeated hits keep the deadline; another enemy replaces the mark. Switching cancels it.

**Augmentation card:**

> Mark an enemy, then move away before the ten-second tax.

### Double Jump

**Weapons:** Dead Calm Dagger

**Item tooltip:**

> Press Jump again in midair after a normal jump to leap once more

**Augmentation card:**

> Press Jump again in midair to jump a second time.

### Echo Salvage

**Weapons:** Echoing Axe

**Item tooltip:**

> A vertical strike that misses living targets but strikes solid ground or a wall stores one echo for 30 seconds. Your next vertical strike that successfully hits an enemy consumes it, adding half your weapon damage, capped at 2/4/6/8 bonus damage. Echoes do not stack; ordinary attacks and circular swings do not consume them. Switching clears the echo.

**Augmentation card:**

> A missed vertical strike stores an echo. Your next successful vertical strike releases capped bonus damage.

### Echoing Speed

**Weapons:** Riftfang Dagger

**Item tooltip:**

> Successful hits on the same enemy each add 5% attack speed, capped at 20/30/40/50% at levels I/II/III/IV. A different target resets buildup. Eight seconds without a hit, switching weapons or the target dying clears it.

**Augmentation card:**

> Build attack speed with repeated hits on one enemy.

### Fire Resistance

**Weapons:** Infernal Claymore, Molten Axe

**Item tooltip:**

> Gain infinite Fire Resistance while holding this weapon in your main hand. Level II also works in the offhand and is the maximum level. Weapon protection ends when neither hand qualifies; other Fire Resistance effects keep their normal duration.

**Augmentation card:**

> Fire Resistance while held; level II also protects from offhand.

### Grave Vault

**Weapons:** Leafcutter Dagger, Overgrown Claymore, Verdant Axe

**Augmentation card:**

> Store flowers in nine slots. Right-click suitable terrain to plant them.

**Augmentation card:**

> Store melee damage as heat; spend heat to ignite your attacks.

**Augmentation card:**

> Quench a burning enemy; stored fire strengthens your next hit.

## Intrinsic weapon passives

**Weapons:** Daggers; knives excluded.

**Weapons:** Axes.

**Item tooltip:**

> Every third attack within 10 seconds slams a 3×3 area ahead.
> Requires an empty offhand.

## Material-hit effects

These are learned separately through augmentation. Each material has one effect; the tooltip substitutes its name, effect strength and weapon-specific duration into the following in-game template:

> On hit: %1$s %2$s for %3$s seconds

The tables below expand that template into the actual English item descriptions for every registered special weapon variant. Roman numerals here are the status-effect strength, not the augmentation rank.
