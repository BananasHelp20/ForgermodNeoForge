# Super augments

A named transformation occurs on the upgrade that reaches the original level IV. The upgraded name then displays level I and can advance through II, III and IV. Sequence: base I, base II, base III, super I, super II, super III, super IV. Stored ranks 1?7 retain the existing ability ID and occupy one slot. Existing transformed rank-IV weapons become super I without losing their effects. This named progression is separate from the Super Augment Template bonus for ordinary abilities.

## Static Field (Direct Current)

Available on all Electrium weapon types and gemstone variants. Super I retains four anchors, barriers and a triangle field. Field pulses once per second for 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 / 1.75 HP at super I?IV. Barrier damage, radius and timing remain as described in game; cooldown stays 60 seconds. Additional field-damage values were chosen conservatively under the user's permission to fill missing progression values. Normal and Super Augment Templates can store and transfer all seven progression steps; neither exceeds super IV.

## Skybreaker (axe Windcharge)

Available on all Skybreaker Axe variants; dagger/claymore Windcharge does not transform. Super I retains the 30-block launch and two-block falling-hit/landing smash. Super II/III/IV multiply the mace fall-damage bonus by 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3, leaving base weapon damage intact. Launch height and 15-second ending cooldown remain unchanged. These additional damage multipliers were chosen conservatively under the user's permission to fill missing progression values. The stored progression is 1?7; templates retain the Skybreaker name and super rank when transferring to an axe.
