Changed
- Ability packets identify the selected hotbar slot and weapon item, so a delayed packet cannot activate a different weapon after a switch
- Canceling Lucid Dreaming removes its Speed effect and restores a preexisting Speed effect when applicable
- Flaming Combo and Rooting Roots cannot be reactivated while their effects are still active
- Ability cooldowns are keyed by the registered weapon item and ability slot, so gemstone variants and primary/secondary abilities have independent deadlines
- Ongoing abilities start their full cooldown when they finish or when switching away from the exact equipped stack cancels them; instant abilities start cooldown on use
- Multiple abilities on the same equipped weapon can remain active together and are canceled independently when their states end
- Taifunite's double-jump charge clears when its dagger is unequipped
- Every special weapon variant now receives independent item properties, preventing Amber fire resistance from carrying over to later registered variants
- Offhand dagger follow-up retains the original target and validates the recent main-hand hit, so knockback or camera movement during the 100 ms delay does not turn a hit into an air swing
- Sudden Presence searches nearby safe landing positions behind hostile mobs, including uneven ground and the next nearest mob if the closest has no safe landing spot
- Ability keys report remaining cooldown or failed activation in the actionbar
