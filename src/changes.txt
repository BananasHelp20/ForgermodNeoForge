# Unreleased changes since Forgermod 0.1.7 (Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge)

## Added
- Added markdown/ability-session-changes.md summarizing this session's implemented ability mechanics, level progressions, super forms, settings, description-only changes and recorded verification; no gameplay changes in this documentation update.
- Reworked Bone Guard/Necromancer groups to last10 minutes, start cooldown on activation and replace the entire old group beside the caster on a ready recast. Groups still fight after weapon switching; expiry, owner death, logout and dimension travel clean up the group and its projectiles. Base Bone Guard rosters remain1 Skeleton /2 Skeletons /1 Skeleton+1 Bogged.
- Added the Necromancer Shift+Alt editor (opens on modifier release, preserving Shift+Alt+S rotation), with4/6/8/10 capacity and twelve non-boss undead choices. Costs: Skeleton/Zombie/Zombie Villager1; Husk/Drowned/Stray/Bogged/Zombified Piglin/Phantom2; Wither Skeleton/Skeleton Horse3; Zoglin4. Skeleton Horse includes its Skeleton rider. Wither and Zombie Horse are excluded. New loadouts default to Skeletons; existing selected rosters are retained when upgrading.
- Replaced Necromancer's fixed rosters, free armor and free enchanted weapons with player-selected reinforcements and, from superII, real mainhand/head/chest/legs/feet equipment per reinforcement. Gear keeps its durability/enchantments across deaths and resummons, never drops from the mob, and is returned to inventory or the player's feet when an entry is removed/replaced. Breaking the controlling weapon also returns banked gear. Mounted entries equip the rider.
- Persistent world SavedData is the single owner of supplied gear; weapons store only bank identity/roster preview, preventing copies from cloning equipment. Live groups lock their bank against editing and simultaneous use by another wielder. Menus validate held weapon/rank/capacity/item slots and prevent moving the controlling weapon. Summon interactions/conversions are blocked to prevent curing, inventory or equipment extraction exploits.
- Reinforcements follow their owner when idle. Native skeleton/melee AI is retained; supplied bows, native enchanted crossbows and non-collectible temporary tridents also work on other undead. Supplied equipment uses player-style armor durability, melee/bow wear and enchantment hooks, including contact-XP Mending with unused XP conserved. Default gear cannot be farmed; summons give no loot/XP, cannot save or portal-clone, and retain friendly targeting, retaliation and player projectile credit.
- Updated Bone Guard/Necromancer descriptions to the approved wording with the actual current roster in white; upgrade previews show capacity and the superII equipment unlock instead of obsolete fixed/free equipment. Added catalog, mounted spawn, menu/capacity/return, persistence/copy lock, durability/enchantment, native ranged AI and activation/recast regressions; updated obsolete lineup/lifetime fixtures.
- Added Blazing Blast I-IV after Explosion IV: same initial explosion/Resistance,4 ghast fireballs at relative cardinal headings and8 blaze fireballs at22.5/67.5/etc-degree headings; projectile damage100/115/130/145% of vanilla and cooldown120/110/100/90 seconds. Projectiles cannot hit their own burst siblings, retain vanilla owner attribution/enchant effects, expire after200 active ticks, cannot persist or portal-clone into unsafe vanilla entities, and keep current white damage descriptions/previews.
- Added server-controlled Explosion terrain damage setting, defaultfalse, shared by base Explosion and Blazing Blast including all projectiles. Disabled means no block destruction or terrain ignition; enabled permits the initial blast to break blocks and projectile impacts to use their normal terrain/griefing behavior. Existing base cooldowns, damage and Resistance are unchanged.
- The Skull Throw natural-flight fixture retains only its already-issued tickets while the accelerated GameTest clock outruns asynchronous chunk activation; native projectile ticks,500-block completion, bounded ticket count and cleanup assertions remain. Runtime Skull Throw code is unchanged.
- Direct Current now marks aimed enemies with successive ability-key uses (not melee hits), uses a50-block aiming limit and retains the50-block connection limit, joins consecutive anchors only and excludes marked anchors, the caster and nonenemies from current damage. Base barriers retain4 damage every0.5 seconds.
- Static Field now fills the area enclosed by all marked positions (independent of selection order), pulses1/1.25/1.5/1.75 damage each second with0.5-second stuns, and never shocks its own marked anchors. Its20-second lifetime starts with the first two-anchor connection and is not refreshed by later marks. Reviewed descriptions and current white values reflect marking, damage, stun and lifetime.
- Stabilized the existing Skull Throw natural-flight regression: it now validates actual completed flight with a bounded800-tick chunk-loading watchdog instead of requiring completion at exactly400 GameTest ticks. No Skull Throw gameplay changes.
- Added Crucible Grappler I-IV after Crucible Hook IV: retains mob pull/smash with2.25/2.5/2.75/3x weapon damage and7/8/9/10-second ignition;25-second cooldown. Aimed block anchors have25/30/35/40-block range, accelerating collision-driven player movement and a synchronized rendered rope. Right-click, switching, destroyed/unloaded anchors and dimension changes release without clearing velocity.

The player's preference travels with the validated ability request, so it also works on multiplayer servers. Reviewed Hook/Grappler damage, burn duration and block range are white; base Hook behavior/cooldowns are retained.
- Challenge Roar now holds initially taunted hostile mobs on the caster for its Strength window, including brain-driven targets and stun restoration; attacks against other entities are prevented during that lock. Switching or losing Strength ends the owned state without removing independent potion effects. Existing base range, Strength and cooldowns are preserved; reviewed range/Strength/duration values are white.
- Added Intimidating Roar I-IV after Challenge Roar IV:35/40/45/50-block range,20/24/27/30-second Strength (+existing Jade bonuses), fixed60-second activation cooldown. A moving Weakness I/Slowness I aura affects enemies entering its radius, clears owned effects on leaving/end, and preserves stronger/longer independent effects. New names, descriptions and upgrade previews reflect the super form.

- Displace now swaps with any visible living target within ten blocks at lower ranks, including passive/allied creatures. At IV+ it can target any visible loaded entity (including items/projectiles) with no fixed distance limit. ## Changed
- Jade gemstone infusion now requires a learned augment that applies a status effect to the user or a target. Jade strengthens each such augment's effects by one level and one second.
- Redesigned the reinforced iron ingot, sword, and block textures.
- Enlarged claymore display models outside the inventory.
- Rebalanced durability, mining speed, attack damage, and mining requirements for mod-metal-made swords/claymores/knifes.
- Reinforced Iron now uses meteoric iron in its recipe; damask and carbon steel use their new intermediate materials.
- Existing gemstone claymores now use their intended effect durations, strengths, and attack attributes.
- Reduced Scrap, Meteoric Iron, Reinforced Iron, and Steel tool damage bonuses to Iron level. Their swords now deal 6 damage, below Diamond's 7.
- Reduced the Damask tool damage bonus by 1, affecting its sword, knife, pickaxe, axe, and hoe; Damask swords now deal 8 damage.
- Reduced Steel pickaxe durability from 1750 to 1200 and mining speed from 9 to 7.5, placing both below Diamond.
- Increased Damask, Titanium, Tungsten, and Carbon Steel mining speeds to 9, 10, 11, and 12.
- Increased Scrap Sword attack speed from 1.4 to 1.6, Reinforced Iron Sword from 1.2 to 1.7, and Steel Sword from 1.6 to 1.75 attacks per second; Meteoric Iron Sword now attacks at 1.65 per second.
