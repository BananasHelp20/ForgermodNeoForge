- Added daggers for all special materials

- Added optional "Create: Enchantment Industry" support: Blaze Enchanters can fill matching templates and Blaze Forgers can apply, extract, and merge augments when that mod is installed.

## Changed
- Jade gemstone infusion now requires a learned augment that applies a status effect to the user or a target. Jade strengthens each such augment's effects by one level and one second.
- Redesigned the reinforced iron ingot, sword, and block textures.
- Enlarged claymore display models outside the inventory.
- Rebalanced durability, mining speed, attack damage, and mining requirements for mod-metal-made swords/claymores/knifes.
- Reinforced Iron now uses meteoric iron in its recipe; damask and carbon steel use their new intermediate materials.
- Existing gemstone claymores now use their intended effect durations, strengths, and attack attributes.
- Reduced Scrap, Meteoric Iron, Reinforced Iron, and Steel tool damage bonuses to Iron level. Their swords now deal 6 damage, below Diamond's 7.
- Reduced the Damask tool damage bonus by 1, affecting its sword, knife, pickaxe, axe, and hoe; Damask swords now deal 8 damage.
- Reduced Steel pickaxe durability from 1750 to 1200 and mining speed from 9 to 7.5, placing both below Diamond.
- Increased Damask, Titanium, Tungsten, and Carbon Steel mining speeds to 9, 10, 11, and 12.
- Increased Scrap Sword attack speed from 1.4 to 1.6, Reinforced Iron Sword from 1.2 to 1.7, and Steel Sword from 1.6 to 1.75 attacks per second; Meteoric Iron Sword now attacks at 1.65 per second.

## Removed
- Removed Scrap Iron crafting and conversion recipes; Scrap Iron items are command-only.
- Removed direct Scrap Ingot-to-Damask and Scrap Iron Ingot-to-Reinforced Iron conversions in favor of the new processing chains.
- Removed Shardium ore smelting and blasting recipes; Shardium now uses its corrected random ore drops.
- Removed gemstone weapon variants and Stumpfl's Bat from creative tabs.
- Expanded the existing Shift+Alt+S ability-order shortcut to cycle all three learned actives left (A/B/C -> B/C/A -> C/A/B -> A/B/C), retaining two-ability swapping. Saved explicit ability order preserves legacy swapped items, upgrades, infusion and extraction without changing ranks, augment counts or passives. Updated the three-slot shortcut hint and message; active-session cancellation and per-ability cooldown identity remain intact.
- Cinder Decoy now disappears into a facing-aligned flame silhouette: flames briefly outline its head, torso, arms and legs before bursting rapidly outward and fading. All detonation paths use the same bounded client effect, sent as one particle emitter packet.
- Changed Pyromaniac to a learned passive: successful melee damage to a mob while burning extinguishes the wielder and heals 2 HP (was an armed active healing 5 HP and transferring fire). Removed its activation, 50-second cooldown, fire transfer and obsolete armed state. Compatible dual-wield daggers share the passive without stacking; updated tooltips and ability documentation.
- Reworked Heating Up: holding the learned passive prevents non-lava fire damage and heals 1 HP every 40/35/30/25 ticks while burning (rank V: 20 ticks). Protection and healing are disabled while in lava, and lava damage is never prevented. Removed melee heat storage, fire absorption, heat-powered ignition/Fire Aspect conversion and the heat tooltip; old stored heat is inert. Recovery resets on extinguishing, unequipping or entering lava. Updated descriptions and regression tests.
- Stepping Through the Void now follows the ground when a downward aim meets the floor, so facing grass no longer rejects the teleport. Swept player collision checks ignore passable vegetation and refine the endpoint to just before walls, ceilings and world limits, including clear distances shorter than half a block. Preserved open-air vertical aiming, camera freedom, 10/20-block range and 7-second cooldown; updated descriptions and tests.
- Ender Swing now immediately performs its damage-and-lift swing when activated, requiring no additional augment or attack input. Removed its armed state and normal-axe-attack trigger; the existing 60-second cooldown starts immediately, including when no target is nearby or the swing breaks the axe. Preserved radius, damage, super-rank bonus, lift clearance checks and offhand restrictions; updated descriptions and tests.
- Deep Wound's successful charged critical now bursts into red fragments and blood-colored dust centered on the struck enemy, including killing hits. The burst is cosmetic and applies no area damage or effects; added a nearby-entity regression check and updated descriptions.
- Fixed Grave Vault's unlimited flower placement in Creative: each successful placement now consumes exactly one stored flower in every game mode, without charging twice in Survival. Manual and kill-triggered placement share live vault storage and synchronize saved/menu counts; failed or cancelled placement remains free. Added repeated depletion and empty-vault/save-load regression coverage for both placement paths with open and closed menus.
- Skybreaker's falling strike and landing smash now affect all mobs, including passive animals, neutral golems and tamed/allied mobs. Preserved player/PvP targeting, owner protection, five-block impact radius, wall checks and existing damage scaling; updated descriptions and all-variant regression coverage.
- Bone Guard and Necromancer reinforcements can now be selected by hostile mobs' normal target AI. Enemies retaliate against the actual summoned melee attacker or archer; arrows retain player ownership for kill credit. Preserved owner/ally protection, friendly iron-golem neutrality and summon cleanup; added proactive-targeting and all-reinforcement retaliation tests.
- Restored Bone Guard's original arrow-helper signature after diagnosing a Necromancer IV stray crash caused by mixed old/new classes in an already-running development game. Shooter metadata is captured before transferring projectile ownership, preserving retaliation and player credit without changing the helper signature. Added a full-group real bow-AI regression against one zombie and fixed cached AI target choices reverting reinforcement retaliation back to the credited player; a real player hit can still reclaim aggro. Fully restart Minecraft after rebuilding to load a consistent set of classes.
- Rebalanced Bone Guard I/II/III to 1 skeleton / 2 skeletons / bogged+skeleton. Necromancer I now summons wither skeleton+bogged+stray; II adds a second wither skeleton; III adds a regular skeleton. Necromancer IV now summons six equipped reinforcements: bogged and stray with Power II bows, skeleton and wither skeleton with Flame bows, wither skeleton with a diamond sword, and skeleton with an iron sword (was seven allies). Equipment selects the correct melee/bow AI. Preserved one-minute lifetime, switching persistence, retaliation, friendly-fire protection and no-loot rules; updated tooltips, lineup documentation and all-rank/AI tests.
- From Necromancer I onward, ranged reinforcements now wear full chainmail and melee reinforcements wear full iron armor, determined by their actual equipped weapon. Bone Guard I-III remain unarmored. Preserved summon lineups, enchanted weapons and no-loot behavior; updated descriptions and added all-slot/all-rank armor and equipment-drop checks across every eligible weapon variant.
